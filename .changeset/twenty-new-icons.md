20 new icons (227 in total), each with 2–3 animation variants: `briefcase`, `newspaper`, `printer`,
`save`, `fingerprint`, `video` and `video-off`, `user-search`, `circle-help`, `circle-play` and
`circle-pause` (a matching pair), `square-check` and `square-x`, `arrow-right-to-line`,
`filter-x`, `folder-up`, `link-off`, `shield-off`, `mail-warning` and `factory`. They join their families
(`circle`, `user`, `filter`, `folder`, `link`, `shield`, `mail`, `arrow`, `video`, and a new `square`).

New in the authoring kit: `snap`, an easing that holds and then jumps on the last frame, for
returning a look-alike end pose to rest unseen (`times: [0, 0.999, 1], ease: ["easeInOut", snap]`).
