`alarm`, `hand`, `map`, `mouse` and `radio`.

New in the authoring kit: `turntable` (real 3D turns of simple solids by crossfading projected poses, and
marks sliding round round solids) and `spin` (keyframes for a flat object spinning on its edge, with a rim).
`parts` gains `ROSETTE`, the scalloped badge outline.
