59 new icons (659 in total): side panels and layouts (`panel-left`, `panel-right` and their open/close forms, `layout`, `layout-panel-top`, `app-window`, `rows`), rich-text controls (`italic`, `strikethrough`, `heading-1`–`heading-3`, `pilcrow`, `case-sensitive`, `remove-formatting`, `wrap-text`, `text-select`, `list-ordered`), and more: `list-checks`, `list-filter`, `square`, `circle-dashed`, `circle-off`, `circle-stop`, `ellipsis-vertical`, `corner-down-right`, `corner-left-up`, `rotate-cw`, `files`, `file-image`, `file-question`, `file-type`, `folder-kanban`, `folder-search`, `folder-tree`, `clipboard`, `archive-x`, `pen-line`, `wifi-off`, `server-off`, `timer-off`, `image-off`, `image-plus`, `unplug`, `signal-high`, `search-x`, `shield-x`, `life-buoy`, `user-minus`, `user-check`, `mail-plus`, `envelope` (a plain envelope without `mail`'s badge), `message-x`, `calendar-x`, `calendar-days` and `dices`.

`hoverTarget: "control"` plays a hover icon from the button, link, menu item or tab around it, and on keyboard focus, instead of only when the pointer is over the icon itself. `reducedMotion: "always"` keeps every icon still, for apps with their own reduce-motion setting. `motion` 14 is now supported alongside 13.
