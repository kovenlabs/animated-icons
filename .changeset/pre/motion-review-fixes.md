Every new icon was watched frame by frame, and about 75 got motion fixes: parts that left the frame (wine, fan, refrigerator, cactus, swords, sticker and more), parts that ran into each other (drama, variable, nfc, panda, grape, plug), a drone that vanished mid-turn, an earth that wobbled out of view, a notebook pen that never touched its line, and sharp-corner artifacts.
