15 new icons, each with 2–3 animation variants: `graduation-cap`, `book-open`, `bus`, `package`, `school`, `bot`, `clipboard-check`, `calendar-clock` and `calendar-cog` (in the `calendar` family), `check-check` (in `check`), `circle-check` and `circle-alert` (a new `circle` family), and `chevron-left`, `chevron-right` and `chevrons-up-down`, which join `chevron-down` in a new `chevron` family.
